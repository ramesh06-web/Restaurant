Our Story

A Table, a Fire & a Philosophy

Ember & Oak was born from a single conviction: that the most honest cooking happens over an open flame. In 2009, Chef Elena Vasquez left a celebrated kitchen in Lyon to return to her roots in the Pacific Northwest, carrying with her a reverence for ingredient and technique that she'd refined across two continents.

Our wood-fired hearth is the heart of everything. We source from fewer than 30 farms within 150 miles, adjusting the menu as seasons turn. What grows together, goes together — and nothing on your plate travelled further than it had to.

  • 🌿
    Hyper-Local Sourcing

    Every ingredient traced to its origin, every producer known by name.

  • 🔥
    Wood-Fire Mastery

    Live fire is our primary cooking medium — no gas, no shortcuts.

  • 🍷
    Curated Wine Program

    200+ labels from small natural producers, thoughtfully paired.
